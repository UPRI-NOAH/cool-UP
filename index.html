Welcome to Cool UP

Beat the heat this El Niño 🌡️

A map of places to cool down across University of the Philippines campuses: shade, greenery, air-conditioned rooms, and drinking water.

    Pins come from OpenStreetMap. Each one is labelled with a provisional cool_up:type tag while the community tagging scheme is finalised. Crowd-sourced contributions are coming soon. Know a spot that's missing? Add it to OpenStreetMap and it will show up here.

    An initiative of UPRI (UP Resilience Institute) and UPRI YouthMappers for El Niño preparedness. Collaboration with UP campuses is ongoing.
